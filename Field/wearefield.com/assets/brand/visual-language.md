FIELD VISUAL LANGUAGE

Field should feel like a scientific instrument.

Not a technology company.

Not a futuristic fantasy.

Not cyberpunk.

Not sci-fi.

Visual references:

* Astronomical maps
* Observatory systems
* Particle simulations
* Neural structures
* Biological systems
* Scientific interfaces

Motion should feel:

* Organic
* Slow
* Alive
* Responsive

Avoid:

* Neon colors
* Blue AI gradients
* Glowing brains
* Robot imagery
* Generic network graphics

The experience should feel contemporary and intelligent.

Not futuristic.

Not nostalgic.

Timeless.
