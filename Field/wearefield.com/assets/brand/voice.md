FIELD VOICE

Field speaks like an observer.

Not like a seller.

Not like a consultant.

Not like a futurist.

Not like a startup.

Tone:

* Calm
* Precise
* Intelligent
* Curious
* Minimal

Avoid:

* Innovation buzzwords
* Marketing clichés
* Corporate jargon
* AI hype language

Prefer:

* Systems
* Signals
* Observation
* Emergence
* Patterns
* Complexity
* Direction

The fewer words, the better.

Every sentence should feel intentional.

The experience should create intrigue before understanding.
