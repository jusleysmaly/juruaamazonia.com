FIELD MANIFESTO

Field is not an agency.

Signals.

Relationships.

Patterns.

Tensions.

Connections.

Field exists to reveal what is hidden.

To transform complexity into direction.

To help organizations see the systems they are already part of.

Visitors should feel that they are observing a living system.

Not a company website.

Not a portfolio.
